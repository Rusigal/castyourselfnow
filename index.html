QUESTIONS

Good to know

I'm not good with technology. Can I do this?

Yes. If you can send a text and check email, you can use this. We go at your pace.

Will the AI submit me to roles?

No. It finds the roles and helps you prepare. You review and click submit yourself, which keeps your accounts in good standing with the casting sites.

Should I cancel my calling service?

That's your call. Calling services submit you directly, and some work well. This gives you your own way to find work, so you're not depending on anyone else.

Online or in person?

Both. Zoom from anywhere, or in person in the Los Angeles area.
